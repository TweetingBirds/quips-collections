### Added
Four quotes added to existing collections:
- **Gratitude & Thankfulness**: `grat-034`, the closing line of Edward Winslow's December 11, 1621 account of the Plymouth harvest celebration ("…we are so far from want, that we often wish you partakers of our plenty."), printed in *Mourt's Relation* (London, 1622), `verified` against Pilgrim Hall Museum's primary-source transcription in modern spelling.
- **Bible Wisdom**: `bible-032`, "Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name." (Psalm 100:4), and `bible-033`, "O give thanks unto the LORD, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever." (Psalm 107:1), both `verified` against the King James Version.
- **Shakespeare**: `shakespeare-055`, Amiens's "Blow, blow, thou winter wind. Thou art not so unkind As man's ingratitude." (*As You Like It*, Act 2, Scene 7, c. 1599), `verified` against the Folger Shakespeare Library text.
