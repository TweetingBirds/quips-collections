### Added
Four quotes added to existing collections:
- **Gratitude & Thankfulness**: `grat-032`, the opening of Lincoln's Proclamation of Thanksgiving of October 3, 1863 ("The year that is drawing towards its close, has been filled with the blessings of fruitful fields and healthful skies…"), `verified` against the Abraham Lincoln Online transcript; the notes record that Seward drafted it.
- **Veterans & Remembrance**: `vets-028`, Woodrow Wilson's message for the first Armistice Day, November 11, 1919 ("…filled with solemn pride in the heroism of those who died in the country's service…"), and `vets-029`, A. E. Housman's "Here dead lie we because we did not choose" (*More Poems* XXXVI, 1936), with the printed word order rather than the common "Here dead we lie". Both `verified`.
- **Sikh Wisdom**: `sikh-020`, Guru Nanak's "conquer your own mind, and conquer the world" (Japji Sahib, Pauri 28, Ang 6), `verified` on SriGranth.org against Sant Singh Khalsa's translation.
