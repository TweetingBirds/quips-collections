## [1.18.1] - 2026-10-20
### Added
Nine quotes added to existing collections, most of them correcting a popular
misattribution:
- **Courage & Conviction**: Mary Schmich's "Do one thing every day that
  scares you," from her 1997 *Chicago Tribune* column, rather than the
  popular Eleanor Roosevelt attribution.
- **Creative Minds**: Blaise Pascal's "I had no leisure to make it shorter,"
  from *Provincial Letters* no. 16 (1656), the source of the line often
  credited to Twain or Franklin.
- **Earth & the Wild**: "Man did not weave the web of life...", credited to
  screenwriter Ted Perry, who wrote it for the 1972 film *Home*, rather than
  to Chief Seattle.
- **Goals & Fresh Starts**: Jim Rohn's "Set a goal to become a millionaire,
  for what it will make of you to achieve it," passing on his mentor Earl
  Shoaff's advice.
- **Love & Romance**: Tolstoy's Levin watching Kitty skate, "seeing her, as
  one does the sun, without looking," in Constance Garnett's translation of
  *Anna Karenina*.
- **On Leadership**: Alfred E. Perlman's "I've done my share," as reported by
  David P. Morgan in *Trains* (July 1983).
- **One-Liners**: Verbal Kint's "The greatest trick the Devil ever pulled..."
  from *The Usual Suspects* (1995), with its Baudelaire antecedent noted; and
  "Insanity is doing the same thing over and over again...", traced to Rita
  Mae Brown's *Sudden Death* (1983) rather than Einstein.
- **Veterans & Remembrance**: George Santayana's "Only the dead have seen the
  end of war," from *Soliloquies in England* (1922), the line MacArthur
  credited to Plato.
